ABOUT RED X

STUDENT ESPORTS CLUB

Red X is a student esports club created for students who share a passion for competitive gaming, teamwork and esports.

OUR BACKGROUND

BUILT FOR STUDENTS

Red X aims to provide a community where students with an interest in esports can connect with one another and participate in esports-related activities.

The club focuses on creating an environment where students can develop teamwork, communication and competitive gaming skills.

A Student Community

Connecting students through esports, gaming and teamwork.

Our Mission

To create an inclusive student esports community that encourages competition, teamwork and personal development.

Our Vision

To build a strong student esports community where students can connect, compete and grow together.
